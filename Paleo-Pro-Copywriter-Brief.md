# Paleo Pro — Copywriter Brief (Website)

This is source material for writing copy for a simple, lightweight marketing
website for the iOS app **Paleo Pro**. It describes what the app is and does so
the copy can be written from scratch. Nothing here is finished copy.

---

## The basics

- **Name:** Paleo Pro
- **Platform:** iOS (iPhone; runs on iPad and Mac via the iOS app)
- **Price:** Free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking.
- **Size / feel:** Small download (~15 MB), fast, fully native.
- **Look:** Rebuilt for iOS 26 using Apple's "Liquid Glass" design language.
- **Current version:** 2.1 (a full rewrite of the original 1.x app).
- **App Store:** https://apps.apple.com/us/app/paleo-pro/id6743683727

## One-sentence description (plain, for reference — not final copy)

Paleo Pro is a free iOS app for learning the ancient Paleo-Hebrew script and
typing in it, with a converter, a system keyboard, a quiz, a reference for all
22 letters, and a library of real inscriptions.

## Who it's for

- People learning the Hebrew alphabet and its history.
- People interested in ancient scripts, paleography, and writing systems.
- Religious / biblical-studies audiences (Jewish, Hebrew Roots, Messianic, and
  general Bible study) where Paleo-Hebrew is a topic of interest.
- Hobbyists who want to type or share text in the old script.

## Background the copywriter should understand

Paleo-Hebrew is the older form of the Hebrew alphabet, used in ancient Israel
before it was gradually replaced by the modern "square script" Hebrew still
used today. It is closely related to the Phoenician alphabet. There are 22
letters, aleph through tav. A few letters take a different "final" form when
they appear at the end of a word in modern Hebrew. The script survives on real
artifacts (inscriptions and coins), some of which are featured in the app.

## What the app does (feature by feature)

Each item below is a plain explanation of the feature so it can be described
accurately. Group or prioritize as needed.

**Converter.** Converts text both directions: modern square-script Hebrew to
Paleo-Hebrew, and Paleo back to modern. It updates live as you type. It can
optionally convert final-form letters to their standard modern equivalents when
they fall at the end of a word (example: 𐤌𐤍𐤑𐤐𐤊 becomes םןץףך).

**Paleo-Hebrew keyboard.** A system keyboard you can turn on and use to type
Paleo-Hebrew in any app on the phone, not just inside Paleo Pro. It can show the
keys labeled in either the Paleo or the modern alphabet so you can find letters
while you learn. (Note for context: the developer is still refining this to feel
more like the built-in keyboard.)

**A page for every letter.** All 22 letters each have their own page showing how
that letter's shape evolved over time into its modern Hebrew form. Any letter or
its evolution can be shared as an image card.

**Learning Center.** A reference section with real historical inscriptions you
can read about, zoom into, and copy the text from, with tappable glossary terms.
Featured artifacts include the Siloam Inscription, the Tel Dan Stele (notable
for the phrase "House of David"), and a silver coin from the First Jewish War.

**Historical letterforms.** A set of fonts based on real scribal hands from
different eras (examples include Ahiram, Gezer, Lachish, Siloam, Qumran, and a
Samaritan hand). You pick one and it is applied throughout the app, including
the keyboard.

**Quiz / game.** A multiple-choice quiz on the alphabet, in both directions
(Paleo-to-modern and modern-to-Paleo). It keeps scores and score history and
tracks which individual letters you tend to get wrong.

**Cheat sheet.** A reference of all letters in either a list or grid view. Each
letter can be copied to the clipboard as the character itself, or as its
Unicode / HTML / Hex code.

**Personalization.** You can set the app's accent/theme color and choose
between alternate app icons.

**Built into iOS.** Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets (such as Letter of the
Day and the letter-evolution view), Spotlight search, Siri Shortcuts, Handoff
between devices, and iCloud sync of your progress across devices.

**Asking for reviews.** The app occasionally shows Apple's standard rating
prompt, only after positive moments (like finishing a quiz with a good score),
never on launch.

## Things worth emphasizing (the differentiators)

- Genuinely free, with no ads and no tracking of any kind.
- Native and lightweight; fast and small.
- More than a converter: it is a learning tool, a reference, a keyboard, and a
  small museum of real inscriptions in one app.
- Covers both the practical (typing, converting) and the educational (history,
  letter evolution, inscriptions).

## Voice / tone guidance

The developer's own posts about the app are plain-spoken and understated, not
hyped. Preferred style for this brand:

- No marketing clichés or "slam-dunk" sales language.
- No em dashes.
- Honest and direct over salesy; let the features speak.
- It's fine to sound personal (this is a solo developer's project).

## What the site is for

Primary goal: get people to download the app from the App Store, and encourage
reviews. Keep the site simple and lightweight (likely a single page). Likely
sections: a short intro, the key features, screenshots, and a download button.

## To confirm before writing

- Whether to mention it also runs on **iPad and Mac**.
- Any **tagline or product positioning** the developer already has in mind.
